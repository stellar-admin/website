Self check-in at Ryokan Hanaya
  1. Collect the key code

    Sent by SMS at 14:00 on the day you arrive.

  2. Open the lockbox by the side gate

    The gate is on Hanamikoji-dori, left of the noren.

  3. Leave your shoes in the genkan

    Slippers are on the rack by the step.

  4. Find your room on the first floor

    Room 3, Sakura. Tea is ready on the table.
