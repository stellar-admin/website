## Usage

```razor
<sa-table>
    <sa-table-caption>...</sa-table-caption>
    <sa-table-header>
        <sa-table-row>
            <sa-table-head>...</sa-table-head>
        </sa-table-row>
    </sa-table-header>
    <sa-table-body>
        <sa-table-row>
            <sa-table-cell>...</sa-table-cell>
        </sa-table-row>
    </sa-table-body>
    <sa-table-footer>
        <sa-table-row>
            <sa-table-cell>...</sa-table-cell>
        </sa-table-row>
    </sa-table-footer>
</sa-table>
```

## API Reference

### `<sa-table>`

The `<sa-table>` tag helper renders a [`<table>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/table)
wrapped in a scrollable `<div>` container. The `class` attribute is applied to
the `<table>` element; other attributes are applied to the container. It has
no attributes of its own.

### `<sa-table-caption>`

The `<sa-table-caption>` tag helper renders a [`<caption>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/caption).
It has no attributes of its own.

### `<sa-table-header>`

The `<sa-table-header>` tag helper renders a [`<thead>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/thead).
It has no attributes of its own.

### `<sa-table-body>`

The `<sa-table-body>` tag helper renders a [`<tbody>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/tbody).
It has no attributes of its own.

### `<sa-table-footer>`

The `<sa-table-footer>` tag helper renders a [`<tfoot>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/tfoot).
It has no attributes of its own.

### `<sa-table-row>`

The `<sa-table-row>` tag helper renders a [`<tr>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/tr).
It has no attributes of its own.

### `<sa-table-head>`

The `<sa-table-head>` tag helper renders a [`<th>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/th),
and forwards standard `<th>` attributes such as `colspan` and `scope`. It has
no attributes of its own.

### `<sa-table-cell>`

The `<sa-table-cell>` tag helper renders a [`<td>`
element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/td),
and forwards standard `<td>` attributes such as `colspan`. It has no
attributes of its own.
